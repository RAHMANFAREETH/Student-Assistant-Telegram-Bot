9) REVISION PLANNER TEST

First make sure at least one upcoming exam exists.

Then:

📚 Revision Planner
→ ➕ Create Revision Plan
→ select exam
→ enter one topic per line

Example:

Introduction
Chapter 1
Chapter 2
Important definitions
Past paper questions

The bot distributes the topics from today until
the day before the exam.


10) TODAY'S REVISION

📚 Revision Planner
→ 📅 Today's Revision

Then:

✅ Complete Revision Task
→ send task number


11) STUDY STREAK TEST

🔥 Study Streak
→ 🎯 Set Daily Target
→ 60

Then complete study sessions through:

⏱ Study Planner

After enough completed minutes:

🔥 Study Streak
→ 🔥 View My Streak


12) STREAK RULE

A day counts when completed study-session minutes
are greater than or equal to the configured daily target.

If today's target is not complete yet, the bot does not
immediately break yesterday's existing streak.
