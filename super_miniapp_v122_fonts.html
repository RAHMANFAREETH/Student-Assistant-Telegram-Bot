Important: legacy font compatibility

Bamini and SunTommy encode Tamil glyphs in Latin characters. Encoded results may look like English until you choose the matching font in Word or design software. Important limitations and privacy

Tamil uses the existing Bamini/SunTommy mappings. Sinhala requires a verified glyph-mapping profile matching your original font (for example FM Abhaya); this tool will not guess glyphs. Arabic Windows-1256 / ISO-8859-6 and English Windows-1252 / Mac Roman are BYTE encodings: for legacy input provide the original .txt bytes. A correctly selected Unicode font such as Sakkal Majalla, Arial or Noto does not require legacy conversion. No text or mapping file is sent to a server.
