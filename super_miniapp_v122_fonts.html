Important: legacy font compatibility

Bamini and SunTommy encode Tamil glyphs in Latin characters. Encoded results may look like English until you choose the matching font in Word or design software. Some rare glyph combinations are ambiguous, and ordinary English embedded in legacy text may convert incorrectly. Proofread before publishing. This tool does not install or provide a font file.
